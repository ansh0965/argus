# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ARGUS to be a project anyone can take part in without being harassed
or made to feel unwelcome - whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Explain your reasoning.
- Give and take feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, say sorry, and move on.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information - an address, a mail, a
  screenshot of their data - without their clear permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer privately, through the contact details on
their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ansh0965). Reports are read, kept
confidential, and acted on. The maintainer may remove comments, commits,
issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban a
contributor temporarily or permanently.
